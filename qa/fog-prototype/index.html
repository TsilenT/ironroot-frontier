Your citadel

Spend 190 gold once. Extra Arrow adds a half-damage shot at a different nearby target. Regeneration restores 3 HP/s after an eight-second purchase and damage wait.

Known ground, local sight

Terrain and both citadel locations are always known. Bright enemies are visible now. Dashed boxes are last-seen buildings, with no current health or production. Unseen troops leave no marker.

Drag the field or tap the minimap to pan. Home returns to your citadel. Front follows your foremost ground troop, or your forward production. Panning never scouts. Volley needs a visible center; damage beyond it does not reveal hidden targets.

Keyboard: arrows pan, +/− zoom, H home, F front, Space pause. Escape cancels an order or closes a panel before pausing. No live reveal or debug controls.

Prototype limits: Classic only, no saves, no campaign, no bridge income. Fog projectiles are suppressed; camera shake is disabled. This checks feasibility, not balance or device performance.
