# One Signal Is Enough: candidate review

Candidate v0.23.0 is based on released v0.22.0 source e0f0a72b68874040f9ea043a57845ef9b68cb80c. This is local readiness evidence, not a claim of remote CI, merge or deployment.

## Player-facing change

Chapter 6 gains a second mission, One Signal Is Enough, after Shared Crossing. Either of two named original signal posts may guide the evacuation. Keep one until 3:30, or defeat the enemy citadel while one still stands. Losing the first is allowed; losing the last or the player's citadel ends the attempt. Saving both and finishing in three minutes are optional medals. This expands the main path to 21 missions and 63 optional stars without changing earlier records.

The normal adaptive enemy spends ordinary income on buildings, upgrades and volleys. Its initial assets and zero treasury are disclosed. There are no hidden combat or income bonuses. Both northern and southern sacrifice routes passed four sampled seeds, and a no-Rootwarden route saved both. These are scripted regression routes, not a human difficulty or balance certification. An additional southern defense started four seconds late lost; that failure is recorded in the validation summary and retained locally.

Named cards preserve the destroyed signal, show the remaining minimum and mark the last signal. Upgrade remains available; original objectives cannot be salvaged or replaced for credit. Restored unknown loss times are labeled rather than invented. Replay now returns to the correct briefing and requires an explicit launch, using the existing ownership/storage protection and preserving its earned-roster versus historical-legacy policy. Unfinished-attempt Restart keeps its exact admitted identity.
