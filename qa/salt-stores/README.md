Numeric design, policies and evaluator were frozen before opening the two heldout seeds. `VALIDATION_PLAN.json` binds all 58 runtime assets, complete materialized code/test source, policies, economy guard, mission evaluator, Salt-specific receipt auditor and native generator. Canonical parent inventory plus the immutable overlay binds the unmaterialized source paths. No numeric tuning occurred in this epoch.

The final executed files are `final-development-epoch1.json` (36 cases), `final-heldout-epoch1.json` (18), `final-receipt-audit-epoch1.json` (54), `validation-epoch1.json`, `final-quickgate-epoch1.json` and `causal-gate-final-epoch1.json`. Earlier quickgate, development and preflight records keep their original executed hashes; they are historical exploration, not the final source binding. The final quickgate was actually rerun and every legal action, AI call, trace, mine event and outcome matched the original. The raw files remain external and are also sealed in the private checkpoint's separate paid-evidence archive.

Exact-state gold-restoration clones are ILLEGAL diagnostic-only counterfactuals. Their snapshots are separate from stripped legal gate receipts and the 54 legal matrix runs. They demonstrate an affordability effect before core damage, not whole-outcome causality. Legal runs have zero unsupported postsetup gold writes and zero ledger error.

The nine policies use player observations only and at most one successful paid action every five seconds. No player spell or citadel purchases are scripted. Plans try the first unoccupied position and wait if unaffordable; destroyed positions may be rebuilt paid. After the plan is occupied, upgrades follow the exact frozen order. Unsupported air and idle have no enforced outcome; their observed losses remain evidence, not acceptance requirements.

`prepare-native.mjs` writes disclosed session-only fixtures outside source and never starts hosting. It refuses source paths and disables service workers. Only app/index receive storage/disclosure wrappers; the other 56 assets stay byte-identical. Native review, remote CI and release approval remain separate.
