# Ironroot Frontier Steam readiness audit

November 14, 2026 · Source baseline `a656ebc` (v0.40.13) · Local polish candidate v0.40.14

**Recommended first release scope:** Windows x64, English interface, single-player, mouse and keyboard with local saves. The campaign has a finale and the registry includes 42 missions, six Field Trials, five optional Guided Practice lessons, and three Skirmish maps. The next release milestone is a private Steam install that can start and resume play. The public browser edition reported v0.40.13 on the audit date.

## Player-facing findings

- **First session.** The Windows v0.40.13 package opened directly on the Campaign briefing. Roadside Watch held time at 00:00 until a paid 140-gold barracks was placed. Combat and recruitment started. The pause menu saved successfully and the title screen offered Resume at 00:15. In Chromium, desktop mouse placement worked at 1280×800, 1280×720 and 960×540. At 800×560 the pause menu needed a scroll to reach Save & return, and the action worked after scrolling.
- **Input and readability.** Keyboard shortcuts can select a building, but keyboard-only play cannot place that building in the main battle. Guided Practice has an existing keyboard-operable placement UI and lacks controller support. Several menu and HUD labels produced sampled capital heights of 6–8 px at 1280×800. If keyboard-only or Deck is a goal, give the battlefield a complete focusable placement and selection path, add usable text scaling, and verify glyphs and default controller coverage in actual hardware play. [Valve hardware compatibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat).
- **Desktop and Deck claims.** Treat Steam Input, Deck compatibility, accessibility feature flags, macOS and Linux as separate verification work. If pursuing Deck Verified, check text legibility at native 1280×800, all default controller paths, any required text entry, and sustained default gameplay on the device. Test interruption and reconnect with a physical controller. [Valve hardware compatibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat), [Steam accessibility features](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/accessibility_features).
- **Cloud and achievements.** Both are optional. If adding Cloud, define account-isolated portable save files with explicit conflict and recovery behavior. The current Chromium storage partition is local to the OS profile. Achievements need their own unlock and offline/retry tests. [Steam Cloud](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/cloud), [Steam stats and achievements](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/achievements).

## Store copy and capture brief

> Build mines, barracks, and siege works, then watch your armies march and fight on their own. Read the pressure on each road, spend gold where it matters, and break the enemy citadel in a 42-mission single-player campaign, six Field Trials, and customizable skirmishes.

Use the same visual identity across the capsules and Steam Library art. Check that the title reads at the small capsule size. Valve lists store header 920×430, small capsule 462×174, main capsule 1232×706, and vertical capsule 748×896. The required library set includes capsule, hero, logo, and header. The client uses a 256×256 shortcut icon and a 184×184 app icon. Take at least five actual gameplay shots at 1920×1080 or larger, 16:9. [Valve graphical assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets), [Store graphical asset details](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard).

Capture one clear shot each of the first paid build, the economy feeding a three-road battle, a defense or bridge objective, archers countering flyers, and a late siege that preserves the finale reveal. Open the trailer with a purchase and the resulting troop wave so the player action is clear with sound muted. Use the in-game HUD in the gameplay footage and allow time for Steam to encode the trailer. [Valve trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer).

Confirm how the Steam offer relates to the public browser edition, then set pricing and the launch description. The runtime's procedural art and music are described in [ART.md](ART.md) and [AUDIO.md](AUDIO.md). Font and packaged Electron/Chromium notices are present. Verify contributor rights and provenance for shipped art, sound, text, localization and marketing media with the owner. The v0.40.14 campaign prose includes wording prepared with AI assistance during this audit. Include that fact in the shipped-content provenance review and complete Valve's general, mature-content and applicable AI disclosures using the final build. [Valve Content Survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey).

Submit the store page before the build and plan at least seven business days for each review. The approved Coming Soon page needs to be public for at least two weeks. Steam Direct applies a 30-day fee-to-release wait to a developer's first few titles. Check the app's actual checklist and dates before promising a launch date. [Valve review](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process), [release process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/releasing), [onboarding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding).
