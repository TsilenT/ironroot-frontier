# The Shared Watch: isolated QA hypothesis, epoch 1

Not admitted, balanced, simulated or released. New design numbers are hypotheses.
This QA-only factory uses the existing full-roster automatic-air/citadel campaign
rules and normal paid adaptive AI. It does not modify any released runtime,
profile, catalog, UI or campaign identity. Bram's cut experiment is not included.
Sparse canonical omissions remain retained files, never deletion candidates.

The choice is a normal paid middle watch with a short shared ground footprint on
both outer roads, versus distributed mobile support that can advance. Both then
attempt a supported core assault. One 240-range Euclidean nearest-target firing
stream is shared, not three batteries. Tower range, economy, caps, ordinary
prices, two-attempt normal AI and 5:00 escalation remain unchanged. A healthy
core has no victory HP floor. Core destruction is the only success condition;
the QA evaluator stops at 600 seconds and gives own-core loss/draw precedence.

Sources: `qa/shared-watch/prototype.mjs` defines the one formation, exact paid
plans and five-second attempted-action cadence. `PLAN.json` preregisters all
24 known cases and operational keep/cut criteria. Both main routes and the replicated shared-defense causal witnesses must succeed on default seed8405 plus another known seed; idle and watch-only must fail on8405 plus another. Isolated stress losses are retained. The opening ends at90s, first original enemy producer loss or first enemy-core damage; own-core pressure and count ratios do not close it. The route plans never repair or
replace lost items, never upgrade the watch, and never inspect enemy cash or
RNG. All rejected commands remain evidence. The exact-cost north placement,
omission and cheaper central archery controls replay successful central-route
commands at their original fixed ticks, with no adaptation or saved-money use.
Watch-only tests whether paying for static cover is sufficient without buying
an assault. Passing scripted cases would demonstrate only known-case causal
feasibility, not difficulty or human success rate.

Importing the runner and aggregate tests never start Shared Watch cases. Raw
runs, source freezes, approval receipts, traces, restore proofs, summaries and
screenshots must stay outside the repo. The runner refuses a reused output
directory and validates its existing parent before creating anything. Source/environment closure is checked before and after every case. Create `OUTPUT_DIRECTORY.stop` to stop within100 fixed ticks; SIGINT/SIGTERM stops between cases after an event-loop yield. Completed cases are retained and the matrix marked incomplete, without automatic retry. Keep all failures; a failed gate cuts this epoch rather than silently
changing a formation, policy, seed or engine rule. No Chapter 9/10 admission,
version bump, profile fence or historical campaign-plan edit belongs in this
prototype checkpoint. Eventual integration needs separate authorization and
compatibility, aggregate, native interaction and release checks on the complete
canonical ancestry.
