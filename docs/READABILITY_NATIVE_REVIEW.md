# Independent native battlefield readability review

Date: 6 November 2026 UTC. Candidate remains private and not Steam-ready.

## Verdict

The candidate produces a real readability improvement, especially at 320-pixel battlefield width. Original silhouettes, outlines, team shape markers, and larger damaged health bars distinguish the five roles better than the frozen renderer. This is more than decorative noise. At the synthetic 140-troop cap, individual bodies and health bars still overlap heavily; this pass does not certify every troop as separately readable.

## Method

Native Chromium in a 1212×886 window, yielding a normal game content viewport of approximately 1180×757. All actual interactions used documented native CUA screenshot-coordinate/mouse/keyboard operations. The accessibility provider reports x11 only, so this is not an assistive-technology review. No DOM evaluation, browser automation driver, hidden state injection, engine mutation, server launch, install, commit or publication.

Responsive pointer fixtures use explicitly sized iframe content viewports of 320×568, 390×650 and 844×390. These are not physical mobile devices. The paired renderer fixture separately supplies exact canvas field sizes (not device viewports) and synthetic frozen poses. It does not simulate played results.

## Scope decision and next quality work

**Accept this as an incremental renderer readability improvement. No blocking regression was observed in this scoped pass.** It should not be called perfect, finished, fully accessible, or Steam-ready.

Nonblocking follow-ups:
1. Maximum-density health strips and bodies still overlap. Explore smarter health display/formation readability in a separately scoped change instead of enlarging everything further. Preserve the now-clear objectives and frontmost silhouettes.
2. Essential action feedback should remain perceptible at the troop cap. Ordinary Rally feedback passed, but a maximum-overlap scene can bury its post-impact ring.
3. Very short practice fields still have small models. The bounded scale change helps but does not replace a properly designed tactical zoom or mobile camera review.
4. Explain redundant team-shape cues in player-facing guidance if users do not naturally infer them; the developer fixture legend is not a shipped usability solution.

## Limits

No physical touch/pen, screen-reader, controller hardware, Safari, subjective audio listening, FPS/frame-time, sustained play, full campaign objective interaction, every held-input transition, or full browser matrix is claimed. Audio controls being present and browser sound activity are not listening evidence. The three sampled practice lessons are not an all-five training rerun. All five silhouettes were compared synthetically; normal play covered warrior, archer, wizard, siege and tracked gryphon through ordinary game/practice flows. Mission badge evidence is the synthetic objective scene, not a played campaign objective. Responsive iframes establish layout/pointer observations only. There was no exhaustive cross-product of every size, theme, effect age and preference.
