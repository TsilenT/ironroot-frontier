This is the local v0.24.0 candidate based on released v0.23.0 source `02fcb4f823c067b4495ea40e93d0a4ceec64b1e6`. It adds one Chapter 6 mission and does not rewrite earlier encounters. It is not yet a publication claim.

## What changed

- `lanterns-on-the-water@1` uses the new, narrowly admitted `frontier-campaign-lanterns@1` identity. Its normal adaptive opponent pays ordinary prices and uses observed contacts and stale memories. The full earned roster remains available.
- Two charted customs houses are known landmarks, not invented sightings. Camera focus grants no vision. HP, levels, upgrades and defenders remain unknown outside sight, with last-seen observations labelled as stale.
- Dispatch explicitly confirms destruction of each original house. Replacements do not reset credit, and resolved originals disappear from presentation memories without rewriting engine knowledge.
- Either original-house clearance or an enemy-citadel victory wins before the 10:00 deadline. Own-citadel loss takes precedence. The existing 5:00 escalation is disclosed. Objective-only terminal views and reports reveal full information without manufacturing an engine winner.
- Profile protocol 15 protects the additive 22-mission/66-star catalog. Protocol 14 clients cannot overwrite new records. Historical battle identities, profile bytes and backed-up progress are retained. The offline shell includes the new presentation module under cache `ironroot-v0.24.0`.
